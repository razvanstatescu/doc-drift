{{PAY:multi-region-epic}} Multi-region active-passive database

Context

All of payments runs in 1 region. A regional outage would stop every Acme merchant from taking payments until the region recovers. Our largest merchants now ask for a recovery time objective of 1 hour in their contracts, and today we cannot promise it.

Decision

We will run the payments database active-passive across 2 regions:

  • The primary region keeps the Patroni cluster as it is today.

  • A standby cluster in the second region follows by asynchronous streaming replication.

  • Failing over to the standby region is a manual, rehearsed decision taken by the incident lead, never automatic.

  • Targets: recovery point objective 1 minute, recovery time objective 1 hour.

Options considered

  1. Active-passive with a manual switch (chosen). Simple to reason about. Asynchronous replication means a regional failover can lose up to about 1 minute of writes, which reconciliation with the PSP can recover.

  2. Active-active. Needs conflict handling for charges and refunds. Too risky for money movement.

  3. Backups restored in another region. Cheap, but a restore takes about 4 hours, which misses the target.

Consequences

  • Infrastructure cost for the database roughly doubles.

  • The failover runbook needs a regional section and a twice-yearly rehearsal.

  • Until this ships, a regional outage is handled as a long incident with the PSP's help. Contracts must not promise a 1-hour recovery time yet.
