How the rotation works

  • Shifts are 1 week, Monday 10:00 to Monday 10:00 CET, with a primary and a secondary.

  • The primary acknowledges pages within 5 minutes. The secondary takes over if the primary does not, and helps on any SEV1.

  • You are not expected to fix everything alone. Escalating early is part of the job.

Before your first shift

  1. Shadow 1 full shift with the current primary.

  2. Do 1 shift as secondary with an experienced primary.

  3. Read every page under Runbooks in this space, starting with .

  4. Run a rollback and a database failover in staging with a teammate watching. Staging is there for this.

  5. Check your access: production cluster read, Vault for payments, the incident tool and the status page.

During a shift

  • Hand over at the start: open incidents, ongoing changes, anything flaky.

  • Keep your laptop and a charger within reach. You do not need to stay home.

  • If you are paged more than twice in a night, the secondary takes the next morning's pages and you start late.

Runbooks are only useful if they are right

If a runbook step did not work as written, say so in the incident review and fix the page the same week. Every runbook has an owner on its first line; tell them if you change something important.

Compensation

On-call weeks are paid as agreed in the on-call policy from People Ops, plus time off in lieu for any night with more than 1 hour of work.
