Welcome

The Platform team keeps Acme's payments running and makes it safe for every other team to ship. We own the payments service, its database, the settlement pipeline and the on-call rotation that looks after them.

Your first week

  • Meet your onboarding buddy. They are your first stop for any question, however small.

  • Get access: Bitbucket, Jira (project PAY), Confluence (this space), the production dashboards and the team channel.

Your first month

  • Ship 1 small change to production, end to end, with your buddy reviewing.

  • Walk through .

  • Shadow an on-call shift. See .

How we work

  • Every change goes through a pull request with 1 reviewer, 2 for anything that moves money.

  • We write things down. If you had to ask how something works, the answer probably belongs in this space.

  • Every page here has an owner. Owners keep their pages current, and anyone can suggest a fix.

  • Incidents are blameless. We look for what the system allowed, not who slipped.
