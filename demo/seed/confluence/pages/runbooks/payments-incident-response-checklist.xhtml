First 5 minutes

  1. Acknowledge the page. If you cannot start within 5 minutes, escalate to the secondary on-call.

  2. Open an incident in the incident tool and pick a severity:

    • SEV1: merchants cannot take payments, or money is moving incorrectly.

    • SEV2: payments work but are degraded: slow, partial errors, delayed settlement.

    • SEV3: a single merchant or a non-critical feature is affected.

  3. Name an incident lead. On a SEV1 the lead does not also debug.

  4. Post a first status update, even if it only says what you know so far.
