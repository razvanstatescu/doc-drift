How a PSP outage looks

  • Charge success rate drops for every merchant at the same time, while our own latency stays normal.

  • Settlement falls behind and worker errors mention timeouts to the PSP.

  • Webhooks stop arriving, or arrive in bursts.

  • The PSP status page may lag by 15 to 30 minutes. Trust our dashboards first.

What to do

  1. Open a SEV2 (SEV1 if charges fail outright). Follow .

  2. Call the PSP support line. The number and our account ID are in the vendor contacts vault entry. Ask for an incident reference and put it in the incident.

  3. Do not roll back or fail over. Neither helps with a PSP outage, and both add risk.

  4. Tell the merchant success team. Merchants see declines with a generic error; they should not retry in a loop.

  5. When the PSP recovers, expect a burst. Scale up first: .

  6. Replay webhooks that failed during the outage: .

Known gaps

We do not yet hold settlement submissions while the PSP is degraded, so workers keep retrying and fill the logs. That work is {{PAY:hold-settlement-psp-degraded}}. Until it ships, expect noisy worker logs during an outage and ignore them.

After the outage

  • Ask the PSP for their incident report and attach it to our review.

  • Reconcile: compare the PSP settlement report for the outage window with our settled charges. Differences go to the finance channel.
