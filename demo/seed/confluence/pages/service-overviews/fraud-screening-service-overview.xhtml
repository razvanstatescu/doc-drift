What it does

Fraud screening scores every charge before we send it to the PSP. It answers in 3 ways: allow, review or block. Payments respects the answer; it never overrides a block.

When screening is down

If screening does not answer within 300 ms, payments fails open for merchants on the low-risk list and fails closed for everyone else. Risk owns the list and reviews it every quarter. A fail-closed period shows up as a drop in charge success rate for some merchants only, which makes it easy to confuse with a merchant integration issue.

Signals

  • Card and device history across Acme merchants

  • Velocity: charges per card, per device and per IP in the last hour

  • Mismatch between billing country and IP country

  • Merchant category and the merchant's own dispute rate

Changing the rules

Rules change only through the Risk team's review. Payments engineers should not add exceptions for a merchant, even during an incident. Ask the Risk on-call instead.
