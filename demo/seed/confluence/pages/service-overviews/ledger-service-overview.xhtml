What it does

The ledger is the double-entry record of every money movement at Acme. Payments tells the ledger what happened; the ledger decides how it is booked. Finance reports, merchant payouts and the monthly close all read from the ledger, never from payments tables.

Rules everyone relies on

  • Entries are append-only. A mistake is fixed with a reversing entry, never an update.

  • Every entry balances: total debits equal total credits, per currency.

  • Amounts are integers in minor units. There is no floating point anywhere in the ledger.

  • The ledger is eventually consistent with payments. A settled charge normally appears within 1 minute; the daily reconciliation catches anything that does not.

Reconciliation

Every night the ledger compares its PSP receivable with the PSP's settlement report. Any difference above 0.00 opens a ticket for the finance team. On-call is only paged if the job itself fails.

Who to ask

Questions about how something is booked go to Finance engineering. Questions about whether a payment happened go to the Payments team first.
