What it does

payments-api takes card charges and refunds from Acme's checkout and merchant tools, sends them to our payment service provider (PSP), and records the result. It is the only service that talks to the PSP. The same image runs in 2 roles:

  • api (payments-api deployment, 3 pods): the HTTP API and PSP webhooks.

  • worker (payments-workers deployment, 2 to 8 pods): settlement. See .

The role comes from PAYMENTS_ROLE, set by the Helm chart.

Configuration

All settings are environment variables, read once at start-up in src/config.ts. Non-secret values come from deploy/helm/values.yaml; secrets come from Vault through the payments-secrets ExternalSecret.

Code layout

Everything lives under src/: server.ts starts the process, routes/ holds the handlers, db/ the connection pool, workers/ the settlement worker. For a guided walk through the code, read .

Dependencies

  • Postgres (payments-pg, Patroni): charges, refunds, idempotency keys, settlement jobs, webhook events.

  • PSP: card authorisation, capture and refunds, over HTTPS.

  • Ledger service: consumes settled charges. See .

SLOs

  • Charge success rate (excluding card declines) at least 99.5% over 30 days.

  • POST /v2/charges p95 under 400 ms.

  • Settlement within 15 minutes for 99% of charges.
