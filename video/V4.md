## 0. What v4 changes, and what it keeps

Razvan on v3 (2026-11-16): the story works and the intro and ending look decent, but the film "looks kind of boring and
cheap" next to Satchel and Pot. Confluence is a plain product to look at, so show fewer Atlassian screens: explain with
crafted visuals, animation and transitions, and prove each point quickly with real recordings or screenshots from the
live product. Try a light version that follows the brand book. Remove Nano Soft from the outro. Improve the intro and
ending a little.

**Keep:** every word of narration, the music, `src/data/timeline.json` (scene spans, cues, markers), the facts, the
real merge of PR #482, the captures, the plates and their credits.

**Change:** the picture. Measured on v3 (R1): the frame is frozen 50.5% of the time and only 25% of frames move
(Satchel 81%, Pot 98%). The product acts are full-width screen recordings on a flat Paper fill.

## 1. Decisions (D39)

1. **A Day film.** The ground is the lit Paper sweep. Night only where it is literal or a turn: the reveal in A0 (black,
   then the Night tile), A1 (3 a.m.) and A4's return to 3 a.m. The light runs: Day (the evidence) → black (the turn) →
   Night (the reveal and 3 a.m.) → the Day slip cut → Day (the product) → Night (3 a.m. again) → a last slip into Day for
   the end card.
2. **Explain with crafted twins, prove with real captures.** A twin is our own UI rebuilt in code with its real words and
   data. Inside the same narration line it hands over to a *proof insert*: a 1 to 2 s crop of a real capture in a white
   mat, tagged "Recorded in Confluence" (or Bitbucket, Rovo, Jira), or "Real output: doc-drift MCP". The 14 inserts R3
   marks *must* are required. This replaces D37's "mock interfaces appear only in the problem act".
3. **End card without a maker line.** It reads "Built on Atlassian Forge for Codegeist 2026" only. Nano Soft stays in
   the video description and on the website.
4. **Motion replaces stillness** as the default (section 2), with type that holds still while it is read.

## 2. Rules (where R1, R2 and R3 disagree, this decides)

### Motion
- **M1. The frame never freezes.** No run of more than 12 identical frames, except the true black at f180 to f194.
  During a reading hold the words stay fixed to their object, and the world keeps moving: the camera creeps (scale +1 to
  2% per second, or 4 to 8 px per second sideways) and the ground motif moves (tide lines 0.5 px per frame, grid
  parallax). `Camera3D` creeps by default; turning it off needs a reason.
- **M2. Read square, move in 3D.** Yaw and pitch up to 12° (15° for an exploded stack) only while the camera travels,
  while a stack explodes or collapses, or inside a transition. Text that is read for 10 frames or more sits within 2° of
  square and at 26 px or more on screen. (R2 showed that a tilted hero at rest looks like a stock mockup.)
- **M3. Arrive by lifting, leave by moving.** Objects rise from elevation 1 to their level over 12 to 15 frames (expo
  out, shadow growing). They leave by a whip, a push or a slip. Opacity fades only for traces, ghosts and dimming.
- **M4. Camera grammar from Pot.** Log-space zoom, pushes sized by duration, expo-out landings on the word, a
  zoom-through on clicks. Parallax: light 0.3, grid 0.5, objects 1.0, floats 1.1 to 1.15.
- **M5. One transition system:** R3's T1 to T11 (section 4 there). In the body, at least twice as many moving transitions
  as hard cuts. Motion blur only on whips and zoom-throughs (5 or 6 samples); check the amber and Paper flats after.
- **M6. Timing.** A visual event lands 0 to 4 frames before the word it illustrates. Cuts and big moments land on beats.
  Body beats are the frames where `frame % 15 === 7` (film frames); bars where `frame % 60 === 37`.

### Look (R2)
- **L1. The ground is lit**, never one flat fill: `sweep()` on Day, `nightSweep()` on Night, the light point moved toward
  the subject.
- **L2. Everything has a height:** `ELEV` 1 to 4, contact shadows, dark objects one step higher (`ELEV_DARK`).
- **L3. One focal element per shot:** a 120 to 300 px statement or number, or one hero object. Labels 26 to 30 px.
  Nothing that must be read is below 26 px on screen. Spline Sans 600 for statements, Spline Sans Mono for literals.
- **L4. Amber means drift.** One amber element per shot, always with its trace and bloom. A highlight on words that are
  not the drift uses the Mist band (multiply) and the Current underline, never amber. The mark at 64 px or less does not
  count. Real captures are exempt (crop so their amber stays incidental). Target: amber in 40% or more of the seconds
  from A2 on (the drift is on screen from V08, and from V14 to V22 the runbook's Steps band stays slipped whenever it is
  in shot).
- **L5. One motif per shot:** the grid on product and precision shots, tide lines on brand and agent moments.

### Proofs (R3 section 3 and R2 section 9)
- **P1.** `ProofFrame`: white mat (outer radius 26, mat 14, inner radius 12), elevation 4, an ink tag chip on its top
  left. Tags: "Recorded in Confluence", "Recorded in Bitbucket: the real merge" (R5), "Recorded in Bitbucket",
  "Recorded in Rovo", "Recorded in Jira", "Real output: doc-drift MCP". The first proof (R1) adds a 26 px note under the
  mat: "Real capture, development install".
- **P2.** At most 1 film px per capture px (captures are 3840 x 2160, 2x CSS). 30 to 60 frames each, never under 24. A
  proof starts on a beat. It morphs from its twin (T3) or sits in front of its twin, which dims to 40%.
- **P3.** Never restyle a proof. Masks from `captures.json` are always drawn. Waits are cut on a beat and logged in
  your scene's README.

### Honesty (R3 section 8, all of it applies)
The byline twin reads "Possibly stale · Doc Drift" (with "· Doc Drift"). The flag tooltip is "PR #482 changed
deploy/helm/values.yaml on Nov 13. Review." P1 suggests only two anchors (`PAY-6` and a Weak `scripts/rollback.sh` that
needs a repository); `deploy/helm/values.yaml` was added by hand. Only the v2 Rovo captures and the D38 answer. Drafts
never touch the page layer. "No AI" only for detection and the rename fix, and it leaves the frame before V21. Merge to
flag is 19 s. The dashboard always carries "Sample data from the demo space". Real names only (Razvan Statescu); never
Dana, Sam, PAY-142, PAY-201 or the "DDS test" pages. Twins never add a control, state, step or word that the real UI
lacks. Bitbucket, Jira and Rovo twins are plain cards that name the product in words, with no product marks and no
Atlassian blue.
